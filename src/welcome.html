TabbySelect is ready

Find what you need in a long list by typing a few letters.

  1. Open a list of choices

    Click a field with a list of choices on a web page to open search. You can also use the Tab key to reach it. If it does not work on a page you already had open, reload the page.

  2. Type what you are looking for

    Type a few letters from the item you want to narrow the choices. For example, typing “pan” can find “Japan”.

  3. Choose and confirm

    Use the arrow keys to choose an item, then press Enter or Tab to confirm. Press Esc to clear what you typed or close the list.

You can start using TabbySelect right away. Choose Open settings to change which pages it works on or what happens when you click. You can also save your settings to a file and load them again later.
